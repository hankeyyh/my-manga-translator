## Why Reading Manga on Pixiv Can Be Difficult

Unlike officially localized manga platforms, most Pixiv manga is published only in Japanese.

Readers often encounter challenges such as:

- Vertical Japanese text

- Handwritten dialogue

- Complex speech bubbles

- Sound effects integrated into artwork

- Multiple dialogue bubbles in a single panel

These elements make generic OCR tools struggle.

---

## Method 1: Download Every Image First

The traditional approach is:

- Download each manga page

- Upload it to an OCR translator

- Read the translated version

- Repeat

Although this works, it has several drawbacks:

- Slow for long chapters

- Requires local storage

- Interrupts the reading experience

- Difficult on mobile devices

For occasional pages it may be acceptable, but it quickly becomes inefficient.

---

## Method 2: Translate Pixiv Manga with Google Lens

Google Lens is often the first tool readers try.

Advantages:

- Free

- Easy to access

- Good for simple screenshots

However, manga presents unique challenges.

Google Lens may struggle with:

- Vertical Japanese text

- Reading dialogue in the correct order

- Multiple speech bubbles

- Embedded sound effects

- Preserving the original page layout
