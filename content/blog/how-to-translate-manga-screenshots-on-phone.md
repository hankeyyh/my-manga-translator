## Why Most Screenshot Translators Fall Short

Many people start with general-purpose image translation tools.

They're great for translating signs, menus, or simple screenshots.

Manga pages are different.

A single page may include:

- Multiple speech bubbles

- Vertical Japanese text

- Handwritten notes

- Sound effects (SFX)

- Complex panel layouts

These elements make manga much harder to process than a normal image.

---

## The Real Challenge Isn't Translation

People often assume the process looks like this:

```
Screenshot
      ↓
Translate
      ↓
Done
```

A dedicated manga translator actually performs several additional steps:

```
Manga Screenshot
        ↓
OCR (Detect Text)
        ↓
Speech Bubble Detection
        ↓
Reading Order Analysis
        ↓
AI Translation
        ↓
Remove Original Text
        ↓
Restore Artwork
        ↓
Natural Typesetting
        ↓
Translated Manga Page
```

Translation itself is only one step.

Keeping the page readable is the difficult part.

## Option 1: Use Google Lens

Google Lens is convenient because it's already available on many Android devices and through the Google app on iPhone.

Pros:

- Free

- Fast

- Good for translating a few words

Cons:

- Doesn't remove the original text

- Often struggles with manga layouts

- Covers artwork with translated text

- Doesn't preserve speech bubbles

It's useful for understanding a panel, but not ideal for reading an entire chapter.

## Tips for Better Mobile Manga Translation

Even with AI tools, a few simple habits can improve the results:

### Use high-resolution screenshots

Higher-quality images make OCR much more accurate.

### Capture the entire speech bubble

Cropping too tightly can remove important context.

### Keep the page upright

Vertical text is easier to detect when the image isn't rotated.

### Translate one page at a time

Most AI tools perform better when processing complete pages rather than partial crops.
